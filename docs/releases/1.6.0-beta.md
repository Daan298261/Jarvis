# ANZU 1.6.0-beta

Testing-team prerelease for Windows desktop and Android.

- Complete animated point-cloud persona bodies and safe framing above the composer. The existing humanoid is preserved.
- Daybreak, Galaxy, Humanoid and Persona avatar in the left Appearance menu; legacy variants removed.
- Explicit desktop command permissions restore Obsidian embedding and related native controls; preserve host size during resize.
- Android live speech, bounded listening, desktop handoff acknowledgment/final replies, and shared desktop projects.
- Recover from unavailable Samsung platform speech providers through installed phone STT or desktop voice.
- Real assistant preferences screen, saved wake opt-in, configurable phrases and aliases, adjustable threshold and clear pause reasons.
- Screen-context consent, complete password-field filtering, and microphone lifecycle fixes.

On current Galaxy S23 One UI, choose ANZU under Settings > Apps > Choose default apps > Digital assistant app, assign Advanced features > Side button > Long press > Digital assistant, then hold the Side button. Older versions may also offer the bottom-corner swipe in Navigation bar settings.

Wake registration and listening are separate: enable listening in ANZU Assistant settings. Audio stays local; listening pauses while the phone is locked, during calls, audio playback and ANZU speech. No microphone starts at boot.

Validation: tests and build results are recorded with the release/PR artifacts. Physical-phone wake-word and gesture testing was omitted at the owner's request; synthetic audio and unit tests do not establish physical-device reliability. This beta is for testing and review, not a declaration of production readiness.
